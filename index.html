Why this exists

Make the exercise of public power easier to verify.

The Citizen's Record is an accountability instrument: a place to trace claims back to sources, compare assertions with records, and keep unanswered questions visible.

We don't ask you to trust the institution. We don't ask you to trust us. We give you a method for checking the record yourself.

Our truth standard

Evidence is not a mood.

We never turn an allegation into a fact because it makes a better headline. When the evidence is incomplete, we say so—and we apply the same standard to citizens, institutions, and ourselves.

Read our standards →
