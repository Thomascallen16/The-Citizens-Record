Mission

The Citizen's Record exists because most Americans encounter the law secondhand — through headlines, soundbites, and campaign ads. We go to the primary source: the bill text, the opinion, the transcript, the filing. Then we explain it plainly, without telling you what to think about it.

Evidence Before Opinion

We show you the document first. Analysis comes second, and it's always labeled as analysis.

Primary Sources Over Headlines

Every claim links back to the bill, ruling, or record it came from.

Politically Independent

We don't endorse candidates or parties. The Constitution isn't a team sport.
